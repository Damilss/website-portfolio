Freelance and contract web work: sites I have designed and built for paying
clients. The brief is usually the same one — a fast, accurate marketing site
that represents the business properly and does not rot after handoff.

## What's in here

- **westcoastbeautyco-com** — a beauty and wellness brand site that replaced a
  Shopify Dawn storefront with a Next.js app, plus a Resend contact form and a
  public Instagram feed.
- **therichmondsquare-com** — a marketing site for a nine-tenant retail plaza in
  Richmond, California, with every user-visible string held in a typed content
  layer.
- **lilianal-com** — a site for a Bay Area mortgage loan originator and realtor,
  built around a unit-tested mortgage payment estimator.
