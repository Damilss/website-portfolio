CSC 101 is Cal Poly's introduction to computer science: variables, control
flow, functions, strings, and lists, worked through in Python. The coursework
was seven labs and four programming assignments, each submitted through GitHub
Classroom with its own unittest suite.

## What's in here

- **county-demographics-cli** — the last and largest assignment: a batch
  interpreter that runs a script of population, percent, and threshold-filter
  queries over 3,143 US county records.

The labs and the earlier assignments live in the course repository rather than
as pages here.
