CSC 202 is Cal Poly's data structures course: the structures that back real
programs — dynamic arrays, linked lists, trees, heaps, hash tables, graphs —
and the algorithms over them, worked through in Python across twelve labs and
projects.

## What's in here

- **csc202-sorting-comparisons** — Lab 6: selection, insertion, and merge sort,
  each returning its comparison count, with a seeded benchmark loop over them.
- **csc202-project5** — the graph project: Kahn's topological sort with cycle
  detection, and a breadth-first two-colorability check.

The remaining labs and projects live in the course repository rather than as
pages here.
